First release.

- Keep-style notes: colored cards, titles, checklists, pinning, archive and search.
- Tags, and a trash that keeps deleted notes for as many days as you set.
- Long press to pick notes, drag to reorder, swipe to archive or pin.
- Quick capture: widget, launcher shortcuts, Quick Settings tile and Share.
- Sync with your memos server as you type, offline until it is back; sign in with a token or the browser.
