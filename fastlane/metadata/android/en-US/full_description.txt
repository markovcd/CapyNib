Capynib is a quick notes app in the style of Google Keep: colored cards, checklists you can tick, pinned notes and an archive.

<b>Your notes stay on your phone.</b> Everything you write is saved on the device first and works fully offline. No account is needed.

<b>Sync with your own server.</b> If you run a <a href="https://usememos.com">memos</a> server, enter its address and an access token and every change syncs about a second after you type. Edits made in the memos web app or on another device come back to the phone. When both sides changed a note, both versions are kept, so nothing you typed is lost.

Each note is one private memo: its title is the memo's first-line heading and its color a tag at the end, so notes read naturally in the memos web app too.

<ul>
<li>Notes with a title, a body and a color</li>
<li>Checklists with items you can tick</li>
<li>Pinning, archive and search</li>
<li>Sync with a self-hosted memos server, offline until the server is back</li>
</ul>
